Career Advice

Expert tips to help you succeed in your job search and career growth in Nigeria

Resume Writing Tips

  • Keep your resume to 1-2 pages maximum
  • Use action verbs like "developed", "managed", "created"
  • Tailor your resume for each job application
  • Include keywords from the job description
  • Quantify your achievements with numbers

Interview Success Tips

  • Research the company thoroughly before the interview
  • Practice common interview questions
  • Prepare thoughtful questions to ask the interviewer
  • Dress professionally (corporate or smart casual)
  • Send a thank-you email within 24 hours

Salary Negotiation Guide

  • Research market rates for your position in Nigeria
  • Know your worth and don't undersell yourself
  • Consider the total compensation package
  • Be confident but professional in negotiations
  • Get the offer in writing before accepting

Networking Strategies

  • Keep your LinkedIn profile updated
  • Attend industry events and tech meetups in Nigeria
  • Connect with professionals in your field
  • Join relevant online communities (Slack, Discord, WhatsApp)
  • Follow up after making new connections
