Career Advice

Expert tips to help you succeed in your job search

Resume Writing Tips

  • Keep your resume to 1-2 pages
  • Use action verbs like "developed", "managed"
  • Tailor your resume for each job
  • Include keywords from the job description

Interview Success Tips

  • Research the company beforehand
  • Practice common interview questions
  • Prepare thoughtful questions to ask
  • Send a thank-you email within 24 hours

Salary Negotiation Guide

  • Research market rates for your position
  • Know your worth and be confident
  • Consider the total compensation package
  • Get the offer in writing before accepting
